# Ambient audio sources

- **shore-waves.mp3**: excerpt from [Gentle small waves lapping on shore.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Alex_hears_things/sounds/352356/) by Alex_hears_things. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Prepared from the publicly available high-quality preview: filtered, compressed, normalized, downmixed, and joined into a 24-second loop with a two-second crossfade.
- **seagull.mp3**: [Western Gull, Golden Gate National Recreation Area](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Western_Gull_Golden_Gate_National_Recreation_Area.ogg), recorded by the US National Park Service. Public-domain US federal government recording. Filtered, normalized, downmixed and faded at the edges; approximately 4.65 seconds.
- **crickets.mp3**: synthesised for this site (no recording): four crickets of different pitch and rhythm, 16 ms syllables on a 4–5 kHz tone with a short room tail, as a seamless 24-second loop. Plays only in night mode, faded in under the waves.

All three files are encoded as mono MP3 at 64 kbps. Sound is on by default, so they load on the visitor's first click, tap or key press (browsers block sound before that), and not at all once the visitor has muted it.
